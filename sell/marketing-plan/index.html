Our Marketing Plan

Our Marketing Plan

Selling well is a marketing job, and we treat it like one. Every Bender Realty listing gets a complete plan built to reach the largest pool of qualified buyers.

Your home is listed on the River Counties MLS and syndicated to the major portals buyers actually use — Zillow, Realtor.com, and hundreds of brokerage sites — with professional photography and compelling copy.

Beyond the portals: targeted social media exposure, our buyer database built over 55 years in this market, agent-to-agent networking across Southeast Tennessee, open houses where they make sense, and yard presence that still matters in a market like ours.

You'll get regular reporting on showings, feedback, and market response — and honest advice if the market tells us to adjust.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where will my home be advertised?

On the River Counties MLS, all major national real estate portals, Bender Realty's website and social channels, and directly to our network of local agents and active buyers.

How important are listing photos?

Critically important — listing photos are your home's first showing. We use professional photography on every listing.

Will you hold open houses?

When they serve the strategy, yes. Open houses work well for certain price points and locations; your agent will recommend what fits your home.
