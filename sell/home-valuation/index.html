Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are online home value estimates?

Automated estimates can miss by 5–10% or more because they can't see your home's condition, updates, or micro-location. A local comparative market analysis is far more precise.

Is a home valuation really free?

Yes. Bender Realty provides complimentary market analyses with no obligation — it's how we begin every seller conversation.

How long does a home valuation take?

We can usually deliver a detailed comparative market analysis within a business day or two of seeing your home.
