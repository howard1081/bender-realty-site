Frequently Asked Questions

What counties does Bender Realty cover?

Bender Realty covers Bradley, Polk, McMinn, Meigs, and Hamilton counties in Southeast Tennessee, with our office located in Cleveland, TN. We also assist clients in neighboring counties throughout the region.

Where is Bender Realty located?

Our office is at 425 25th Street NW, Cleveland, TN 37311 — call (423) 472-2173 or stop by during business hours.

Often, yes. We regularly assist clients in adjoining Southeast Tennessee counties and can refer you to a trusted partner anywhere else in the state.
