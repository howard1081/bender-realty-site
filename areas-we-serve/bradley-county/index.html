Living in Bradley County

Bradley County is the heart of Bender Realty's service area and home to our Cleveland office. Anchored by Cleveland — one of Tennessee's fastest-growing small cities — the county blends established in-town neighborhoods, newer subdivisions, rural acreage, and easy access to I-75, Chattanooga, and the Ocoee region.

Buyers are drawn to Bradley County for its strong schools, Lee University, a healthy job base, and home prices that remain reasonable compared to the Chattanooga metro. Whether you want a historic home near downtown Cleveland, new construction near Hopewell, or land toward Charleston, our agents know every corner of this county.

Communities in Bradley County

Cleveland

Cleveland is the county seat of Bradley County and the hub of Southeast Tennessee living — a growing city of roughly 50,000 known for Lee University, a revitalized downtown, strong schools, and quick access to both Chattanooga and the mountains.

Learn More →

North Cleveland

North Cleveland covers the neighborhoods north of downtown toward Charleston and I-75 exit 27 — an area popular for newer subdivisions, convenient shopping along Paul Huff Parkway, and some of the county's most requested school zones..

Learn More →

South Cleveland

South Cleveland stretches from downtown toward the Hamilton County line, offering established neighborhoods, larger lots, and quick access to APD-40 and Highway 64 toward Chattanooga and Ocoee..

Learn More →

Charleston

Charleston sits on the Hiwassee River at Bradley County's northern edge, a small historic town with riverside character, rural land, and a location minutes from I-75 — a favorite for buyers who want space without giving up convenience..

Learn More →

Hopewell

Hopewell is a fast-growing community in northern Bradley County where newer subdivisions meet farmland.

Learn More →

McDonald

McDonald sits along the Bradley–Hamilton county line, putting Chattanooga, Ooltewah, and Cleveland all within an easy drive.

Learn More →

Black Fox & Wildwood Lake

Black Fox and Wildwood Lake are established neighborhood areas on Cleveland's south side, offering mature trees, a mix of mid-century and newer homes, and quick access to APD-40 and South Lee Highway..
