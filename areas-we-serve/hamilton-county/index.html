Living in Hamilton County

Hamilton County is the greater Chattanooga market — from riverside city neighborhoods to mountaintop communities and the fast-growing Ooltewah–Collegedale corridor next door to Bradley County. Bender Realty helps clients on the northeastern side of the metro, where our Cleveland base gives us a natural edge.

Chattanooga's job growth, outdoor lifestyle, and national attention keep demand high across Hamilton County. From Ooltewah and Apison to Soddy-Daisy and Signal Mountain, our agents help buyers and sellers navigate one of Tennessee's most dynamic markets.

Communities in Hamilton County

Chattanooga

Chattanooga is Tennessee's scenic city — a revitalized riverfront downtown, thriving neighborhoods like North Shore and Southside, and an outdoor lifestyle few cities can match.

Ooltewah

Ooltewah is one of the Chattanooga metro's fastest-growing communities, halfway between Chattanooga and Cleveland along I-75.

Apison

Apison is a countryside community southeast of Ooltewah near the Georgia line, where new subdivisions meet horse farms and rolling pasture.

Collegedale

Collegedale is home to Southern Adventist University and a family-friendly mix of established neighborhoods, new construction, and the popular greenway system connecting to Ooltewah..

Harrison

Harrison sits on the eastern shore of Chickamauga Lake, offering lakefront homes, Harrison Bay State Park, and an easy commute to Chattanooga, Ooltewah, or Cleveland..

East Ridge

East Ridge borders Chattanooga along the Georgia line, offering some of the metro's most affordable single-family homes, a central location off I-75, and ongoing reinvestment along Ringgold Road..

Red Bank

Red Bank is a close-in Chattanooga suburb just north of downtown, popular with first-time buyers and renovators for its mid-century housing stock, small-business corridor on Dayton Boulevard, and five-minute drive to the North Shore..

Soddy-Daisy

Soddy-Daisy stretches along Chickamauga Lake north of Chattanooga, with lakefront and lake-access homes, mountain backdrops, and a small-town feel inside the Hamilton County school system..

Signal Mountain

Signal Mountain rises above Chattanooga with brow-view homes, top-ranked schools, and a historic town center.

Lookout Mountain

Lookout Mountain is one of Chattanooga's most storied addresses — historic estates, brow lots overlooking the valley, and the landmarks of Rock City and Point Park..

Walden

Walden occupies the quieter, wooded top of Signal Mountain, where larger lots, brow views, and the town's small commercial core create a private mountain community minutes from Chattanooga..
