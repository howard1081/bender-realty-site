Living in Meigs County

Meigs County is one of Southeast Tennessee's best-kept secrets — a quiet, rural county bordered by Chickamauga Lake and Watts Bar Lake on the Tennessee River. Lakefront property, farmland, and small-town homes in Decatur define the market here, often at prices well below neighboring counties.

With two major lakes, no interstate congestion, and a location within an hour of Chattanooga and Knoxville, Meigs County attracts lake buyers, retirees, and anyone looking for land with water access. Bender Realty covers the county from Decatur to Ten Mile.

Communities in Meigs County

Decatur

Decatur is the county seat of Meigs County, a small town surrounded by farmland between Chickamauga and Watts Bar lakes.

Learn More →

Ten Mile

Ten Mile sits at the northern end of Meigs County on Watts Bar Lake, a favorite for lakefront homes, boat-dock properties, and weekend retreats — with some of the most affordable big-water frontage in East Tennessee..

Learn More →

Big Spring

Big Spring is a rural Meigs County community between Decatur and Birchwood, known for rolling farmland, long-range views, and larger tracts of land near the Chickamauga Lake side of the county..

Learn More →

Birchwood

Birchwood straddles the Meigs–Hamilton county line near the Tennessee River, famous for its sandhill crane refuge.

Learn More →

Georgetown

Georgetown sits where Meigs, Bradley, and Hamilton counties meet, giving residents an easy drive to Cleveland, Ooltewah, and Chattanooga.

Euchee & Watts Bar Lake

The Euchee area fronts Watts Bar Lake in northern Meigs County, near the Euchee boat ramp and marina.
