Living in Polk County

Polk County is Southeast Tennessee's outdoor playground — home to the Ocoee River, the Cherokee National Forest, and the mountain towns of the Copper Basin. Real estate here means riverfront cabins, mountain acreage, hunting land, and small-town homes in Benton, Ocoee, Copperhill, and Ducktown.

Because so much of Polk County is national forest, private land is limited — which makes local knowledge essential when buying or selling here. Bender Realty helps clients navigate mountain properties, unrestricted acreage, and recreational land throughout the county.

Communities in Polk County

Benton

Benton is the county seat of Polk County, a small town at the foot of the Chilhowee Mountains along Highway 411.

Ocoee

Ocoee is the gateway to the Ocoee River — world-famous whitewater and the 1996 Olympic venue.

Learn More →

Copperhill

Copperhill sits on the Tennessee–Georgia line in the historic Copper Basin, sharing a walkable downtown with McCaysville, GA.

Learn More →

Ducktown

Ducktown is a historic Copper Basin mining town surrounded by the Cherokee National Forest.

Learn More →

Conasauga

Conasauga is a quiet rural community in southern Polk County near the Georgia line, where properties tend toward farmland, timbered acreage, and country homes well off the beaten path..

Learn More →

Delano

Delano lies along the Hiwassee River corridor in northern Polk County, known for riverfront land, farms, and easy access to Highway 411 between Benton and Etowah..

Learn More →

Farner

Farner is one of Tennessee's most remote communities, tucked into the mountains of upper Polk County near the North Carolina line — a place for cabins, creekside land, and true seclusion..

Reliance & Cherokee National Forest
