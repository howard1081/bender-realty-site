Making a Strong Offer

When the right home hits the market in Cleveland or Bradley County, well-priced listings can move fast. A strong offer is about more than price — terms, timing, and presentation all matter.

Your Bender Realty agent will run a comparative market analysis so you offer from evidence, not emotion. We look at recent nearby sales, condition, and days on market to find the number that wins without overpaying.

Beyond price, sellers weigh earnest money, financing strength, inspection timelines, and closing dates. Sometimes matching the seller's preferred closing date beats a higher price from a slower buyer.

We prepare every offer to present you as the buyer the seller wants to work with — pre-approved, organized, and ready to close.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much earnest money is typical in Cleveland TN?

Earnest money in the Cleveland area commonly runs about 1% of the purchase price, though it varies by price point and competition. It's credited back to you at closing.

Can I make an offer contingent on selling my current home?

Yes, home-sale contingencies are common — but they weaken an offer in competitive situations. Your agent can help you structure timing, or explore buying first with bridge options.

Should I waive the inspection to win a house?

We rarely recommend waiving inspections. There are safer ways to strengthen an offer — shorter inspection windows, larger earnest money, or flexible closing dates.
