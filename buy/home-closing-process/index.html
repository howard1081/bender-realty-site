The Closing Process

The Closing Process

From accepted offer to keys in hand usually takes 30–45 days in Tennessee. Knowing what happens at each step keeps the process smooth and surprise-free.

After contract acceptance, you'll deposit earnest money, schedule inspections, and finalize your loan application. Your lender orders an appraisal while a title company searches the property's title and prepares closing documents.

The inspection period is your window to identify issues and negotiate repairs or credits. Your Bender Realty agent handles these negotiations and keeps every deadline on track.

At closing, you'll review and sign the final documents, funds are disbursed, and the deed is recorded. Tennessee is an attorney/title-company closing state, and we coordinate with all parties so closing day is a celebration, not a scramble.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does closing take in Tennessee?

Most financed purchases close in 30–45 days from contract. Cash purchases can close in as little as one to two weeks.

What are closing costs for buyers in Tennessee?

Buyer closing costs typically run 2–5% of the purchase price, covering lender fees, title insurance, taxes, and prepaid escrow items. Your lender provides an itemized estimate up front.

Do I need a final walk-through?

Yes — we always schedule a walk-through within 24 hours of closing to confirm the home's condition and that any negotiated repairs were completed.
