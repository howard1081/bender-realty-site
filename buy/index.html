Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I use a buyer's agent?

A buyer's agent represents your interests — not the seller's. Your Bender Realty agent advises on pricing, negotiates on your behalf, coordinates inspections, and manages every deadline through closing.

How do I start the home buying process?

Two first steps: talk with a lender about pre-approval, and talk with a Bender Realty agent about what you're looking for. We'll handle the rest step by step.

Does Bender Realty work with first-time buyers?

Constantly — and we enjoy it. We explain every step in plain language and connect you with programs like THDA loans that can lower your costs.
