Frequently Asked Questions

What are your management fees?

Fees depend on property type and portfolio size. Contact our property management office for current fee schedules — we'll put everything in writing before you commit.

Who holds the security deposit?

Security deposits are held in accordance with Tennessee law and accounted for at move-out with a documented condition comparison.

What if my tenant stops paying rent?

We follow a firm, legally compliant process: notices, communication, payment plans where appropriate, and coordination of eviction proceedings when necessary.

Can I use my own contractor?

In most cases yes — tell us your preferred vendors and approval limits, and we'll coordinate around them.

How do you set the rental rate?

We run a rental market analysis using comparable leased properties in your area — priced to minimize vacancy while maximizing income.
